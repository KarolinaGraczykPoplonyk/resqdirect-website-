# How Much Does Car Recovery Cost in Peterborough? UK Prices Compared (2026)

**Vehicle Recovery Price Guide | Peterborough, Cambridgeshire and the UK**

Your car has broken down in Peterborough. Perhaps you are stranded near the A1, have developed an engine fault on the A47, or need a non-running vehicle transported from your driveway to a garage.

One of your first questions will probably be: **How much will recovery cost?**

The answer depends on more than distance. Vehicle size, actual weight, collection time, loading difficulties and the type of recovery equipment required can all influence the final price.

Some Peterborough recovery operators advertise starting prices below £100. However, these figures are not necessarily what every customer will pay.

This guide examines publicly advertised recovery prices in Peterborough, explains how operators calculate charges, compares selected prices with London and helps drivers understand what to ask before accepting a quote.

## 1. How Much Does Car Recovery Cost in Peterborough?

Published prices from selected recovery operators serving Peterborough show that basic services can start at approximately **£45–£80**, depending on the company.

These are advertised entry prices, not a verified average cost of recovery in Peterborough.

For example, a company advertising recovery from £50 may charge more for a longer journey, heavier vehicle, night-time collection or specialist loading.

The price for a straightforward local recovery can therefore differ substantially from the cost of transporting a large SUV or immobilised van across the country.

## 3. What Is Included in a Recovery Price?

A recovery quote may include several different elements.

### Call-out or minimum charge

This can cover the cost of dispatching a recovery vehicle and attending the collection location.

Some operators apply a minimum charge even for very short journeys.

### Mileage

The distance travelled can significantly influence the price.

However, operators may calculate chargeable mileage differently.

Some calculate mileage between the collection and delivery points. Others may account for the journey to reach the stranded vehicle or the return journey.

Always ask which distance is being charged.

### Loading and unloading

A vehicle that starts, rolls and steers normally may be easier to load than one with locked wheels, accident damage or a seized gearbox.

Additional equipment or loading time may affect the price.

### Vehicle type

A heavier SUV, large van or unusual vehicle may require equipment with greater carrying capacity.

### Time of collection

Night-time, Sunday and bank-holiday charges may differ from standard daytime rates.

### Additional services

Winching, difficult access, waiting time and storage may incur additional charges.

The final price depends on the terms of the individual quotation.

## 4. Why Does Vehicle Size and Weight Affect Recovery Costs?

Recovering a small hatchback is not necessarily the same job as transporting a large SUV or commercial van.

The recovery operator must consider the vehicle's actual weight, dimensions, condition and loading requirements.

A larger vehicle can require greater carrying capacity, more suitable securing equipment or a different recovery truck.

### Small, medium and large vehicle comparison

| Vehicle category | Examples | Recovery considerations |
|---|---|---|
| Small car | Fiat 500, Ford Fiesta | Generally lighter and often suitable for standard car recovery equipment |
| Medium car | Ford Focus, VW Golf | Weight and dimensions must still be checked |
| Large car or SUV | BMW X5, Volvo XC90 | Higher weight may limit suitable recovery vehicles |
| Large van | Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter | Dimensions, wheelbase and carrying capacity become especially important |
| Loaded commercial vehicle | Van carrying tools or materials | Actual loaded weight must be assessed before collection |

These categories are examples rather than official recovery pricing classes.

Two vehicles that appear similar in size can have different weights.

### Why recovery truck capacity matters

The Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency requires recovery operators to comply with permitted gross weights, axle weights and relevant train-weight limits.

A recovery vehicle cannot legally carry an oversized or overweight load simply because the vehicle fits onto its bed.

For a transporter, available payload depends on the recovery vehicle's permitted weight and its actual operating weight, including relevant equipment, fuel and occupants.

A heavier vehicle may therefore require a different recovery arrangement.

This can affect availability and operating costs.

**Vehicle weight does not automatically mean a fixed surcharge.** It matters when the weight or dimensions change what is needed to carry out the job safely and legally.

When requesting a quote, provide the exact vehicle make, model and, for vans, whether the vehicle is loaded.

## 6. Is Car Recovery More Expensive at Night or on Weekends?

It can be.

Some recovery companies apply different prices for night-time, Sunday or bank-holiday work.

Others advertise fixed starting prices regardless of the time of day.

There is no single standard night-time surcharge across the UK.

### Peterborough recovery charges

MIDO's Recovery indicates that late-night surcharges may apply in certain circumstances.

The company does not publish one fixed surcharge covering every recovery.

Drivers should therefore request a specific quotation rather than assume the advertised minimum charge will apply at any hour.

### Why might out-of-hours recovery cost more?

Operating a recovery service outside standard hours can involve additional staffing, scheduling and availability costs.

However, the actual surcharge depends on the company's pricing policy.

Before booking, ask whether the quoted total includes all relevant out-of-hours charges.

## 9. What About Breakdown Recovery on the A1, A47 or A15?

A breakdown on a major road can create different challenges from a vehicle that has stopped safely on a private driveway.

The collection location, traffic conditions, available access and safety requirements can all affect how a recovery is arranged.

### If your vehicle breaks down on a motorway

Follow National Highways' safety advice.

If you can, move to an emergency area or another suitable place of safety. Use your hazard warning lights and follow the appropriate motorway breakdown procedures.

If you are in immediate danger, call 999.

If you cannot safely leave your vehicle or reach a safe location, follow emergency-service guidance.

Do not attempt to arrange loading or vehicle repairs in an unsafe traffic position.

### What information should you provide?

Tell the recovery operator:

- The road and direction of travel.
- Your precise location, using a location marker or safe location-sharing method.
- Whether the vehicle is in a live traffic lane.
- Whether passengers are in a safe location.
- The vehicle's condition.
- Where the vehicle needs to be transported.

Roadside safety takes priority over comparing prices.

## 10. Does a Non-Running Vehicle Cost More to Recover?

Sometimes.

A vehicle that will not start but rolls and steers normally may be relatively straightforward to load using suitable equipment.

A vehicle with locked wheels, seized brakes, accident damage or restricted access may require more time or specialist equipment.

### Factors that can increase loading complexity

- Locked wheels.
- Electronic parking brake faults.
- Missing keys.
- Gearbox stuck in park.
- Flat or damaged tyres.
- Suspension damage.
- Very low ground clearance.
- Vehicle positioned in a confined space.

Not every recovery operator can handle every situation with the same equipment.

Provide accurate information when requesting a quote.

## 11. Is It Cheaper to Have Breakdown Cover?

Breakdown-cover membership and private vehicle recovery are different services.

A breakdown policy may include roadside assistance, local recovery, onward travel or national recovery, depending on the policy selected.

However, cover can have conditions, exclusions and limitations.

For example, not every basic policy includes recovery to any destination in the UK.

Private recovery is usually quoted for a specific vehicle movement or breakdown incident.

### Which is better?

Breakdown cover may be suitable for drivers who want ongoing protection against future incidents.

A private recovery operator may be useful when a vehicle is not covered, when a particular transport job is needed, or when the customer wants a separate quotation.

Always check the policy terms before assuming a recovery is included.

## 12. How Can You Avoid Unexpected Recovery Charges?

Before accepting a quote, ask the company to confirm:

1. The total price for the job.
2. Whether the call-out charge is included.
3. How mileage is calculated.
4. Whether the vehicle's size or weight affects the price.
5. Whether night-time, weekend or bank-holiday charges apply.
6. Whether VAT is included, if applicable.
7. Whether loading or winching costs are included.
8. Whether waiting time or storage could cost extra.
9. Whether the quoted price covers delivery to your chosen destination.
10. What circumstances could change the agreed price.

Where practical, ask for the quotation in writing.

The goal is not simply to find the lowest advertised starting price.

It is to understand the total cost of recovering your particular vehicle.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

### How much is car recovery in Peterborough?

Selected operators advertise starting or minimum charges from approximately £45–£80. The final cost depends on distance, vehicle type, time and recovery requirements.

### How much does recovery cost per mile?

Rates vary. MIDO's Recovery advertises £2 per mile, while Tow Away Recoveries lists £2.50–£4.20 per mile. Their charging structures and minimum fees are different.

### Is recovering an SUV more expensive than a small car?

It can be. Larger or heavier vehicles may require different carrying capacity or equipment, although there is no universal SUV surcharge.

### Does it cost more to recover a van?

It may, particularly if the van is large, heavily loaded or requires a higher-capacity recovery vehicle.

### Is recovery more expensive at night?

Some companies apply out-of-hours charges. Others may not. Ask for the total price before booking.

### Is Sunday recovery more expensive?

It depends on the operator. Some companies include Sundays in their higher-rate periods.

### Can I get a fixed quote for recovery from Peterborough to London?

You can request a quote for the exact journey, but the operator will usually need the collection address, destination, vehicle details and condition.

### Do recovery companies charge for travelling to me?

Some may account for travel to the collection point or return mileage. Ask exactly how the chargeable distance is calculated.

### Does a non-running car cost more to transport?

Not always. A non-running car that rolls and steers can be easier to load than one with locked wheels or significant damage.

### Is vehicle recovery the same as vehicle transport?

Recovery generally involves moving a vehicle following a breakdown or incident. Planned transport involves moving a vehicle between locations, such as an auction collection or garage delivery. The equipment may overlap, but the circumstances and pricing can differ.
